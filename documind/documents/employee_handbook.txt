# NOVATECH SOLUTIONS
## Employee Handbook
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2026

*Note: Novatech Solutions is a fictional company. All policies below are sample data for software development and RAG testing.*

---

## 1. Company Overview

Novatech Solutions is a technology company headquartered in Pune, Maharashtra, India. The company provides software development, cloud computing, and artificial intelligence solutions.

Our standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday through Friday.

Employees are expected to maintain professionalism, respect colleagues, and protect company information.

## 2. Leave Policy

All full-time employees are eligible for the following annual leave benefits:

| Leave Type | Annual Entitlement |
|---|---:|
| Casual Leave (CL) | 12 days |
| Sick Leave (SL) | 10 days |
| Earned Leave (EL) | 15 days |
| Public Holidays | 10 days |

### 2.1 Casual Leave
- Employees receive 12 casual leave days per calendar year.
- Casual leave can be taken for personal matters.
- Employees should apply at least 2 working days in advance.
- Unused casual leave cannot be carried forward.

### 2.2 Sick Leave
- Employees receive 10 sick leave days per year.
- A medical certificate may be requested for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive working days.
- Employees should notify their manager as soon as possible.

### 2.3 Earned Leave
- Employees receive 15 earned leave days annually.
- Earned leave can be carried forward up to a maximum balance of 30 days.
- Leave exceeding the carry-forward limit will lapse.

### 2.4 Maternity and Paternity Leave
- Eligible female employees receive maternity leave according to applicable Indian law.
- Male employees receive 10 working days of paternity leave.
- Paternity leave must be used within 6 months of the child's birth.

## 3. Work From Home Policy

- Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week.
- Remote work requires prior manager approval.
- Employees must remain available during core working hours (10 AM to 5 PM).
- Employees working remotely must ensure they have a stable internet connection.
- New employees in their first 30 days must work from the office unless an exception is approved.

## 4. Attendance Policy

- Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM.
- Employees are allowed a grace period of 15 minutes.
- More than 3 late arrivals in a calendar month may result in a discussion with the manager.
- Employees must record attendance through the company's HR portal.
- Any attendance correction request must be submitted within 3 working days.

## 5. Salary and Payroll

- Salaries are paid on the last working day of each month.
- Salary is transferred directly to the employee's registered bank account.
- Employees must submit their bank details and tax declarations during onboarding.
- Salary slips are available through the HR portal.
- Payroll-related queries should be sent to payroll@novatech.example.

## 6. Probation Policy

- New employees undergo a probation period of 6 months.
- During probation, performance and conduct are evaluated by the reporting manager.
- The company may extend probation by up to 3 months if performance expectations are not met.
- Confirmation of employment is communicated through an official email.
- Probationary employees are eligible for casual and sick leave.

## 7. Performance Reviews

- Performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in June and December.
- Reviews consider technical performance, communication, teamwork, ownership, and goal completion.
- Salary revisions and promotions depend on performance, business requirements, and management approval.
- Employees are encouraged to maintain quarterly performance goals.

## 8. Employee Expenses

Employees may claim reimbursement for approved business expenses.

| Expense Category | Maximum Reimbursement |
|---|---:|
| Internet Allowance | ₹1,000/month |
| Mobile Allowance | ₹500/month |
| Local Travel | ₹3,000/month |
| Client Meals | ₹2,000 per meeting |

- Expense claims must include valid receipts.
- Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense.
- Manager approval is mandatory.
- Personal expenses are not reimbursable.

## 9. Code of Conduct

Employees must:
- Treat colleagues with respect.
- Avoid discrimination and harassment.
- Maintain confidentiality of company and client information.
- Avoid conflicts of interest.
- Use company equipment responsibly.
- Follow applicable laws and company policies.

Any form of workplace harassment should be reported to HR.

## 10. Information Security

- Employees must never share company passwords.
- Multi-factor authentication must be enabled on company accounts.
- Confidential documents must only be shared with authorized individuals.
- Company data must not be stored on personal cloud accounts without approval.
- Security incidents must be reported immediately to the IT department.

## 11. Resignation and Notice Period

- Permanent employees must provide 60 calendar days' notice before resignation.
- Employees must submit resignation through the HR portal.
- Managers may approve an early release depending on business requirements.
- Employees must return company assets before their final working day.
- Final settlement is processed within 45 days of the employee's last working day, subject to applicable clearances.

## 12. Company Holidays

The company observes 10 public holidays annually. The official holiday calendar is shared by HR at the beginning of each calendar year.

Employees working on designated public holidays may be eligible for compensatory leave, subject to manager approval.
